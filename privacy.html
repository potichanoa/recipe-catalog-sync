Privacy Policy — Recipe Catalog Sync

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Recipe Catalog Sync is a personal project for organizing recipe Pins saved by its owner on Pinterest.

Information accessed

If the owner authorizes the Pinterest integration, the application may access the Pinterest information covered by the permissions granted during authorization, such as Pins and boards needed for the recipe catalog.

How information is used

Authorized Pinterest information is used only for the personal purpose of organizing recipe Pins into a recipe catalog, identifying existing Pins, and adding newly discovered recipe information to that catalog.

Sharing

The project is intended for personal use. It does not intentionally sell, rent, or share authorized Pinterest information with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Data storage and deletion

The project is designed to keep the recipe catalog under the owner's control. The owner may delete locally stored catalog data and may revoke the application's Pinterest authorization through Pinterest.

Changes

If the project's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are used.

Contact

This is a personal project. For questions about this policy, contact the project owner through the repository or project page where this policy is hosted.
