Recipe Catalog Sync

A personal recipe organization project

What is this?

Recipe Catalog Sync is a personal tool being developed to organize recipe Pins saved by its owner on Pinterest into a searchable recipe catalog.

The planned Pinterest integration is intended for personal use. It may read authorized Pinterest Pins and boards, identify recipes that are new to the catalog, and help organize recipe links and related information.

Purpose

The project is for personal recipe organization and experimentation with the Pinterest API. It is not a Pinterest replacement and is not affiliated with or operated by Pinterest.
